EVIO - PEER-TO-PEER ELECTRIC VEHICLE CHARGING GRID & MOBILITY PLATFORM
DETAILED FEATURES LIST AND PLAIN-ENGLISH SYSTEM EXPLANATION
================================================================================

1. WHAT IS EVIO?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EVIO is a complete peer-to-peer (P2P) electric vehicle charging marketplace and 
energy mobility network designed for modern EV drivers, private/commercial charging 
hub hosts, and electrical grid supervisors.

In simple terms:
- If you drive an EV, EVIO helps you find chargers, book guaranteed charging slots, 
  plan long journeys so your battery never dies, order swappable batteries to your 
  breakdown location, or get emergency roadside charging from nearby EVs on highways.
- If you own a charger, home wallbox, or commercial property, EVIO lets you list your 
  chargers, charge visiting drivers, manage bookings, review real-time electricity 
  consumption (kWh), and earn money.
- If you are a grid supervisor or electrical inspector, EVIO gives you full root 
  authority to audit earthing safety certificates, balance power feeder loads, approve 
  or delete non-compliant stations, and issue wholesale hardware quotations for new 
  franchise hubs.


================================================================================
2. USER ROLES & DASHBOARDS
================================================================================
EVIO supports three distinct, role-tailored dashboards:

1. DRIVER DASHBOARD:
   For EV car and 2-wheeler owners who need to discover stations, book slots, 
   plan road trips, order battery delivery, or request roadside emergency charging.

2. HUB (HOST) DASHBOARD:
   For charging station owners and operators to manage their charging nodes, 
   approve/reject driver booking requests, inspect live electrical power (kWh), 
   and download past tax invoices.

3. SUPERVISOR DASHBOARD:
   For electrical safety administrators and grid operators to oversee all chargers 
   across the network, verify compliance certificates, monitor transformer loads, 
   and manage wholesale franchise partnerships.

3. DRIVER DASHBOARD FEATURES IN DETAIL
================================================================================

A. INTERACTIVE LIVE MAP & CHARGER DISCOVERY
- Real-time Leaflet OpenStreetMap with full zoom, pan, and GPS geolocation.
- Instant "Use GPS / Locate Me" button that drops your vehicle pin at your real location.
- Color-coded pins on the map:
  * Green Pins: Community Hub stations with advance slot booking available.
  * Purple Pins: Pre-existing public fast charging stations (Tata Power, Ather, Jio-bp, Statiq).
  * Amber Pins: Stations currently in-use by another driver.
  * Grey Pins: Offline or maintenance stations.
- Interactive popups showing station address, power rating (kW), per-unit price in Rupees (₹), 
  connector types, and direct "Book Slot" or "Start GPS Directions" buttons.

B. OPEN CHARGE MAP API INTEGRATION
- Fully integrated with the live global Open Charge Map API (API Key: ad006700-30f2-4b9a-a092-38e1f21c3234).
- Fetches and displays verified public fast charging stations around your current coordinates 
  and along expressways.
- Clearly flags public stations as "DRIVE-IN ONLY" so drivers know they operate on a 
  first-come, first-served walk-in basis without advance slot booking.

C. DYNAMIC JOURNEY PREDICTION & TRIP PLANNER
- Complete road trip energy simulation tool:
  * Origin and Destination Inputs: Enter any starting place and destination (e.g. Pune, Mumbai, 
    Lonavala, Goa, Delhi, Bengaluru, etc.).
  * Automatic Geocoding: Automatically resolves coordinates for any town, city, or address, 
    and calculates accurate highway road distance.
  * EV Vehicle Selection: Choose from popular models with factory specs:
    - Tata Nexon EV Max (40.5 kWh, 138 Wh/km)
    - Tata Tiago EV (24 kWh, 110 Wh/km)
    - MG ZS EV (50.3 kWh, 145 Wh/km)
    - Mahindra XUV400 (39.4 kWh, 140 Wh/km)
    - Hyundai Ioniq 5 (72.6 kWh, 155 Wh/km)
    - Ather 450X Gen 3 (3.7 kWh, 35 Wh/km)
  * Current Battery (SoC %) Slider: Adjust your battery level from 10% to 100%.
  * Driving Dynamics Mode: Switch between ECO (+12% range), NORMAL, and SPORT (-22% range).
  * Cabin Climate (AC) Toggle: Factoring in auxiliary air-conditioning power consumption.
  * Discharge Prediction Engine: Simulates battery consumption km-by-km. If the battery is 
    predicted to die before the destination, it triggers an alert warning with the exact 
    kilometer mark where the car will stop.
  * Smart Charging Stop Recommendation: Recommends the optimal fast charging hub before 
    your battery hits critical reserve, showing arrival battery %, required charge duration, 
    kWh boost up to 80%, estimated cost in ₹, and a 1-click "Book Slot" button.
  * Interactive Route Map: Visualizes the full route, Origin (Pin A), Destination (Pin B), 
    Recommended Charging Stop (Zap Pin), and the Critical Discharge Point (Warning Beacon).
  * Telemetry Graph: Displays an SVG battery degradation curve (SoC % vs Distance in km).

D. ADVANCE CHARGING SLOT BOOKING
- Book charging slots at community hubs in advance to guarantee an available parking bay.
- Choose duration (1 to 4 hours) and start time.
- Transparent pricing breakdown showing energy rate (₹/kWh), total estimated cost, 
  and platform fees.
- Two booking modes:
  * Instant Booking: Immediate confirmation without waiting for host.
  * Host Approval: Sends a request to the hub operator; confirmed once the host accepts.

E. REAL-TIME CHARGING SESSION TELEMETRY
- Live charging session dashboard once plugged in:
  * Real-time flow rate in kilowatts (kW).
  * Energy delivered counter in kilowatt-hours (kWh).
  * Battery percentage progress ring.
  * Live meter running cost in Indian Rupees (₹).
  * Emergency Stop button to immediately conclude the session.

F. DOORSTEP SWAPPABLE BATTERY DELIVERY
- For vehicles with removable or quick-swap batteries (e.g., 2-wheelers and light EVs):
  * Click "Battery Delivery" from the top navigation bar or quick banner.
  * Interactive map automatically locates you via GPS.
  * System calculates Haversine distances to all nearby hubs and automatically assigns 
    the closest hub operator with fresh battery stock in inventory.
  * 4-stage tracking timeline: Order Placed ➔ Technician Dispatched ➔ Arriving at Vehicle ➔ Battery Swapped.

G. HIGHWAY V2V REVERSE CHARGING SOS (PEER-TO-PEER)
- For stranded EV drivers on highways who run out of battery between distant stations:
  * Connects with active online EV drivers who have bidirectional V2L / V2V charging 
    capability and have the EVIO website open.
  * Interactive radar map showing your stranded vehicle location and nearby cruising 
    drivers on the same highway corridor.
  * Send emergency roadside energy beacon to a passing driver to request 5 kWh to 15 kWh.
  * Live status showing transfer speed (7.2 kW to 11 kW) and driver phone contact.

H. ACTIVE EV DRIVER HEATMAPS & CHARGING DESERTS
- Switch map view to Heatmap mode:
  * High Density EV Clusters (Green/Amber): Areas with heavy EV commuter traffic.
  * Charging Deserts (Red): Unserved dark zones where zero fast charging stations exist, 
    highlighting prime locations for new franchise hubs.

I. IN-APP WALLET & TAX RECEIPTS
- Built-in wallet with balance top-up in Indian Rupees (₹).
- Downloadable and viewable past tax invoices for every completed charging session, 
  including station details, GST, power consumed, and session time.

4. HUB (HOST) DASHBOARD FEATURES IN DETAIL
================================================================================

A. CHARGING FLEET MANAGEMENT
- Dedicated operator console showing all charging stations registered by the host.
- KPI cards tracking Total Fleet Count, Combined Grid Capacity (kW), Net Earnings (₹), 
  and Total Sessions Dispensed.

B. TRUE REAL-TIME POWER TELEMETRY (kWh)
- Accurately tracks electrical power delivered across the host's charging bays.
- Zero-Charger Fix: If a host has not listed any chargers yet (0 stations), all stats 
  strictly read 0 kWh, 0 sessions, ₹0 earnings, and a clean empty state card is displayed:
  "No Charging Nodes Connected — List your first charger to start streaming telemetry."
- When chargers exist, power delivery graphs calculate actual kWh delivered from 
  confirmed and active charging sessions.

C. LIST NEW CHARGER WIZARD (WITH INTERACTIVE PINNING MAP)
- Easy 4-step wizard to add any charger (home, hub, office, or commercial):
  * Step 1: Location & Pinning Map: Click on an interactive Leaflet map to drop an 
    exact GPS pin for your charger, or use browser location. Select premise type 
    (Home Driveway, Commercial Hub, Office Tech Park).
  * Step 2: Electrical Specifications: Power capacity (kW), fast/rapid/slow tier, 
    connector types (Type 2, CCS-2, CHAdeMO, GB/T, Type 1), cable length, and simultaneous bays.
  * Step 3: Pricing & Operating Schedule: Rate per unit (₹/kWh), 24/7 or custom hours, 
    instant booking vs host approval required.
  * Step 4: Electrical Safety Compliance: Upload earthing verification certificate, 
    inspection report, and photos of the charging bay.

D. DRIVER REQUEST AUTHORIZATIONS & AUTO-ACCEPT SWITCH
- Incoming booking requests from drivers appear in the "Driver Requests" queue.
- Hosts can review vehicle model, requested time slot, and click "Accept" or "Decline".
- Auto-Accept Toggle: Hosts can enable "Auto-Accept Requests" so bookings are automatically 
  confirmed without manual intervention.

E. STATION INSPECTION MODAL & PAST RECEIPTS
- Click on any listed charger in your fleet to open the full inspection modal:
  * Live status (Available, In-Use, Offline).
  * Power delivery history and weekly usage charts.
  * Complete list of past driver receipts with transaction IDs, energy dispensed, 
    and earnings breakdown.
- Toggle Vacation Mode to temporarily hide the station from the driver discovery map.
- Delete Charger button to permanently remove the node from the system.

5. SUPERVISOR DASHBOARD FEATURES IN DETAIL
================================================================================

A. ROOT ADMINISTRATIVE AUTHORITY & DELETION CONTROL
- Grid Safety Supervisors have full administrative oversight of every charging node 
  on the network.
- Supervisors can inspect any station, review its technical compliance, or permanently 
  delete any unsafe or non-compliant charging station from the network.

B. ELECTRICAL SAFETY & EARTHING AUDIT QUEUE
- New charger submissions uploaded by hosts enter the supervisor's pending queue.
- Supervisors inspect the uploaded earthing test reports (verifying earth resistance < 5 Ohms, 
  RCD Type B trip curve, and circuit protection).
- One-click "Approve & Authorize Live" to publish the station onto the public map, 
  or "Reject" with specific feedback to the host.

C. REGISTERED HUB OPERATORS DIRECTORY
- A dedicated directory tab listing all commercial hub operators and hosts.
- Click on any hub operator to view their complete charging station fleet, combined 
  kilowatt capacity, live status, and historical session logs.

D. SUBSTATION FEEDER LOAD BALANCING
- Real-time transformer load and grid line saturation monitoring (kW).
- Live station load table showing rated power and operational status.
- Grid frequency stability and voltage variance telemetry.

E. FRANCHISE WHOLESALE PRICING & QUOTATION PIPELINE
- When prospective hub operators submit a franchise application to buy commercial chargers 
  in bulk, the application is routed to the Supervisor Console.
- Supervisors review the applicant's proposed location (viewable on an interactive map) 
  and electrical sanction load.
- Supervisors formulate custom wholesale rates (₹ per charger) based on order volume, 
  add verification notes, and dispatch the quotation directly to the applicant.

F. DISPUTES & ARBITRATION QUEUE
- Driver-host charging dispute handling (e.g., interrupted sessions, faulty connectors).
- Supervisors review evidence and can issue full or partial wallet refunds with 1 click.

6. FRANCHISE BULK PROCUREMENT SYSTEM
================================================================================
- Allows individuals and commercial property owners to establish their own multi-charger 
  EVIO Hub.
- EVIO aggregates bulk orders directly from tier-1 manufacturers (ABB, Schneider Electric, 
  Delta, Exicom) to secure wholesale volume discounts.
- Transparent Application Workflow:
  1. Applicant chooses charger types (7.4kW AC, 22kW Dual AC, 60kW DC Fast, 120kW Superfast) 
     and quantity.
  2. Applicant drops a pin on an interactive Leaflet map to select the proposed hub site.
  3. Fixed rates have been removed; upon submission, the application is sent directly to 
     the Grid Supervisor.
  4. The Supervisor verifies the site and provides an official wholesale price quotation (in ₹).
  5. The applicant reviews the quotation on their dashboard and proceeds with setup.
